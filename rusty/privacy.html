Rusty Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

Developer: Mykyta Ovsiannikov

Contact: f1xyegb@gmail.com

In short: Rusty collects no data. Your progress lives only on your device. The developer never receives anything. There are no accounts, servers, analytics, or ads.

1. Data processed

The app keeps only what it needs to remember your learning: completed lessons and exams, answers to review, points, hints and boosters, streaks, and settings such as language, theme and vibration. All of this is stored on your device, inside the app's protected container.

2. What we don't do

  • We don't send data to the developer or any third party. The app's only network traffic is the App Store, to show the price of the full version, complete a purchase and restore it.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers, or third-party SDKs.
  • No access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photo library or health data is requested.
  • We never sell or share data.

3. Purchases

The full version is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details, name or e-mail. Whether you own the full version is read from your Apple Account through StoreKit.

4. Reminders

If you allow notifications, Rusty schedules local reminders on your device (for example, when your daily streak is about to break or topics are due for review). They are created by the app on the phone; nothing is sent to a server. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications → Rusty.

5. Keychain copy and backups

To keep your progress when you reinstall the app, Rusty also stores a copy of it in your device's Keychain. This copy stays on your device (and in iCloud Keychain only if you use it); the developer has no access to it. «Reset progress» in the app's Settings replaces it with empty progress.

If iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) is enabled, app data may be included in your backup. This is an Apple system feature governed by Apple's privacy policy; the developer has no access to it.

6. Deleting your data

Use «Reset progress» in the app's Settings to erase your progress, including the Keychain copy; deleting the app removes the rest of its data on the device. No deletion request to us is needed because we hold no copy of your data. A purchase stays with your Apple Account and can be restored after reinstalling.

7. Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

When this policy changes we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be noted in the App Store release notes.

9. Contact

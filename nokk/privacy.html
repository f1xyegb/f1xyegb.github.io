nokk Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 September 2026

Developer: Mykyta Ovsiannikov

Contact: f1xyegb@gmail.com

In short: your data lives on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. The developer never receives it. There are no accounts of our own, no servers, analytics, or ads.

1. Data processed

The app stores only what you enter: habit and task names, completion marks, categories, photos you attach, reminder times, and appearance settings. All of this lives on your device, inside the app's protected container — and, when iCloud is on, in your iCloud (see section 4).

2. What we don't do

  • We don't send data to the developer or any third party. The app's only network traffic is iCloud sync between your devices and your own iCloud account, and the App Store for subscriptions.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers, or third-party SDKs.
  • No access to location, contacts, camera, photo library, or health data is requested. Photos you attach to a task are chosen through the system picker and stored with the rest of your data.
  • We never sell or share data.

3. Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device using iOS system facilities. You can revoke permission in iOS Settings → Notifications → nokk.

4. iCloud sync and backups

When you are signed in to iCloud, nokk keeps a copy of your data in the private database of your iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit) so it survives reinstalling the app and appears on your other devices. This data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple, is tied to your Apple Account, and is accessible only to you: the developer has no way to read, query, or receive it. Turning iCloud off for nokk in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud stops the sync; the app then works with the copy on the device.

If iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) is enabled, app data may also be included in your backup. Both are Apple system features governed by Apple's privacy policy; the developer has no access to either.

5. Deleting your data

Delete the app and its data on the device is removed with it. The copy in your iCloud can be deleted in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → nokk. No deletion request to us is needed because we hold no copy of your data.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes

When this policy changes we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be noted in the App Store release notes.

nokk

Habits and tasks on one road through your day: close a habit by holding its ring, a task with a tap on its circle.

Close a habit or a task

Press and hold a habit's ring for about a second and a half — the screen inverts while you hold and snaps back when it's done. A task closes with a tap on its circle.

Move a task to tomorrow or delete it

Swipe a task to the right to move it to tomorrow, to the left to delete it.

nokk Pro

nokk Pro is a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription with a 7-day free trial. To manage or cancel it, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On a new device open Settings in nokk and tap Restore Purchases.

Your entries on another device

With iCloud turned on for nokk in iPhone Settings, your entries are kept in your own iCloud account and appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

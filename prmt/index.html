prmt

A bug journal for vibe coding, on iPhone and in the Mac's menu bar: collect bugs, changes and ideas with screenshots, then copy them as one prompt for your coding agent.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: f1xyegb@gmail.com. We usually reply within two days.

Getting started

  • iPhone: tap + on Projects to make a project, then add notes with Paste, Photos or the Share sheet. Copy at the bottom of a project puts every note and image on the clipboard as one prompt.
  • Mac: prmt lives in the menu bar. Click its icon: the Overview opens, with every project in the sidebar. Paste a screenshot with ⌘V or drop it on a project, then click Copy (⌘↩). Right-click the icon for Settings and Quit.

Share to a new project

In the Share sheet (iPhone) or Share menu (Mac), choose prmt, then + or New Project… and type a name: the project is made together with the note. If prmt is missing from the Mac's Share menu, choose Share → Edit Extensions… and turn prmt on.

Sync between iPhone, iPad and Mac

Sync comes with prmt Pro: notes sync through your own iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple Account on each device and keep iCloud on for prmt; the Mac's Settings show the iCloud status. Without Pro, notes stay on the device they were made on.

Restore Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase that works on iPhone, iPad and Mac. On a new device open Settings in prmt (on the Mac: right-click the menu bar icon → Settings…) and choose Restore Purchases.

Paste doesn't work

Copy the text or image again in the other app, then paste in prmt. The clipboard is read only when you paste.

prmt Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

Developer: Mykyta Ovsiannikov

Contact: f1xyegb@gmail.com

In short: your notes live on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. The developer never receives them. There are no accounts of our own, no servers, analytics or ads.

1. Data processed

The app stores only what you enter: project names and briefs, notes, the images you add to them, and your settings. All of this lives on your device, inside the app's protected container — and, when iCloud is on, in your iCloud (see section 5).

2. What we don't do

  • We don't send data to the developer or any third party. The app's only network traffic is iCloud sync between your devices and your own iCloud account, and the App Store for the optional Pro purchase.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers or third-party SDKs.
  • No access to your photo library, camera, location or contacts is requested. Images reach prmt only when you paste them, pick them in the system photo picker or a file dialog, drop them on the Mac app, or send them with the Share menu.
  • We never sell or share data.

3. Clipboard

prmt reads the clipboard only when you paste (Paste on iPhone, ⌘V on the Mac), and writes to it only when you copy. What you copy goes wherever you paste it; that app's own privacy policy applies there. On the Mac, the images of a copied prompt are saved as files in the prmt folder inside your Downloads, so they can be pasted as attachments; images deleted in prmt are removed from that folder the next time it starts.

4. Share extension

Text and images you send to prmt from other apps on iPhone or Mac wait in the app's shared container on that device until prmt files them into a project.

5. iCloud sync and backups

When you are signed in to iCloud, prmt keeps a copy of your projects and notes in the private database of your iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit), so they appear on your iPhone and your Mac and survive reinstalling the app. This data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple, is tied to your Apple Account, and is accessible only to you: the developer has no way to read, query or receive it. Turning iCloud off for prmt stops the sync; the app then works with the copy on the device.

If iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) is enabled, app data may also be included in your backup. Both are Apple system features governed by Apple's privacy policy; the developer has no access to either.

6. Deleting your data

Delete a note, a project or the archive in the app, and it is deleted on all your devices. Deleting the app removes its data from that device. The copy in your iCloud can be deleted in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → prmt. No deletion request to us is needed because we hold no copy of your data.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

When this policy changes we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be noted in the App Store release notes.

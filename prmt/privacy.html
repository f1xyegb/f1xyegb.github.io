prmt Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

Developer: Mykyta Ovsiannikov

Contact: f1xyegb@gmail.com

In short: prmt collects no data. Your notes, images and settings stay on your device. The developer never receives them. There are no accounts, servers, analytics or ads.

1. Data processed

The app stores only what you enter: project names and briefs, notes, the images you add to them, and your settings. All of this lives on your device, inside the app's protected container.

2. What we don't do

  • We don't send data to the developer or any third party. The app's only network traffic is the App Store, for the optional Pro purchase.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers or third-party SDKs.
  • No access to your photo library, camera, location or contacts is requested. Images reach prmt only when you paste them, pick them in the system photo picker, or send them with the Share sheet.
  • We never sell or share data.

3. Clipboard

prmt reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste, and writes to it only when you tap Copy. What you copy goes wherever you paste it; that app's own privacy policy applies there.

4. Share extension

Text and images you send to prmt from other apps wait in the app's shared container on your device until prmt opens and files them into a project.

5. Backups

If iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) is enabled, app data may be included in your backup. This is an Apple system feature governed by Apple's privacy policy; the developer has no access to it.

6. Deleting your data

Delete a note, a project or the archive in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from the device. No deletion request to us is needed because we hold no copy of your data.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

When this policy changes we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be noted in the App Store release notes.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: f1xyegb@gmail.com.

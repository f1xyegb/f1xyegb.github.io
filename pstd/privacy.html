pstd Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

Developer: Mykyta Ovsiannikov

Contact: f1xyegb@gmail.com

In short: everything you put into pstd stays on your device. The developer never receives it. There are no accounts, no servers of ours, no analytics, no third parties and no tracking. The one exception is a server of your own, if you choose to connect one (section 8).

1. Data processed

pstd stores only what you enter: projects, networks, posts with their pictures, captions, dates and times, and appearance settings. All of it lives on your device, in a local database inside the app's protected container.

2. What we don't do

  • We don't send data to the developer or to any third party. pstd has no server of ours and no account.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers, or third-party SDKs.
  • pstd does not connect to Instagram, TikTok or Threads and does not post for you.
  • We never sell or share data.

3. Photos

pstd asks for add-only access to your photo library. It is used only when you tap Save, to put a picture from pstd into your library. pstd cannot read the photos already in your library. Pictures you add to a post are chosen through the system picker or the Share sheet and are kept with the rest of your data.

4. Calendar and Reminders

Full access to Calendar and Reminders is requested only when you turn the integration on. pstd creates, updates and removes items only in its own "pstd" calendar and its own "pstd" list; it does not change anything else. You can turn the integration off in pstd Settings, or revoke access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.

5. Notifications and Live Activity

If you allow it, pstd schedules local notifications and shows a Live Activity on your device using iOS system facilities. Nothing is sent through a server. You can revoke permission in iOS Settings → Notifications → pstd.

6. Share extension

When you choose pstd in the Share sheet, the extension passes what you shared (a picture, text or a link) into pstd. This happens on the device and nothing leaves it.

7. nokk bridge

Only if you turn it on, pstd exchanges data about scheduled posts (title, first line of the caption, date, time and whether it is done) with the nokk app on the same device. The exchange goes through a shared app group on the device; it does not use the network.

8. Your own server (Claude)

Only if you connect a server that you own (Settings → Claude), pstd sends that server your project names, networks, profile handles, posting slots and scheduled dates, and takes back the posts (text, pictures, videos) that your own Claude assistant queued there. The connection is encrypted (HTTPS) and goes only to the address you scanned and confirmed. The developer runs no part of it and receives nothing. The server deletes a post and its files once pstd has taken them. Tap Disconnect on the same screen to stop; what is on your server is yours to delete.

9. Backups

If iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) is enabled, app data may be included in your backup. This is an Apple system feature governed by Apple's privacy policy; the developer has no access to it.

10. Deleting your data

Delete the app and its data on the device is removed with it. No deletion request to us is needed because we hold no copy of your data. Items pstd added to your "pstd" calendar and list can be removed in the Calendar and Reminders apps.

11. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

12. Changes

When this policy changes we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be noted in the App Store release notes.

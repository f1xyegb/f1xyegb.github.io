pstd

Plan your posts: a profile grid for each project and network, with the pictures and captions at hand.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: f1xyegb@gmail.com. We usually reply within two days.

Projects and networks

Each project has its own profile grid for Instagram, TikTok and Threads. Add a project, pick the networks you post to, and switch between them inside the project.

Reorder the grid

Press and hold a post in the grid, then drag it to a new place. The other posts move aside to make room.

Calendar

Pull the calendar down to unfold it from a week into the whole month. Drag it back up to fold it.

Posting times and notifications

Give a post a date and a time and pstd reminds you when it is due, with a notification and a Live Activity on the Lock Screen. Both can be turned off in iPhone Settings → Notifications → pstd.

Add from Photos or Safari

In Photos or Safari tap Share and choose pstd. Pick the project and what you shared is added to it, so you can plan it later.

Calendar, Reminders and nokk

In pstd Settings you can turn on three integrations: Calendar and Reminders show your scheduled posts in your own "pstd" calendar and list, and nokk adds them as tasks in the nokk app on the same iPhone. Each one is off until you turn it on.
